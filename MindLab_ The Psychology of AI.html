Debrief

What did MindLab show us?

🎨

Attention

Habits like reading run on autopilot and can hijack what we are trying to do.

🧠

Memory

We remember what we attend to and what we can group. Memory is limited, not a recording.

🔍

Bias

First impressions and expectations quietly steer our judgements.

🤖

AI trust

Confident is not the same as correct. Check, compare, then decide.

Questions for the class

  1. Which game surprised you most, and why do you think your brain reacted that way?
  2. Think of a time you were sure about something and turned out wrong. What happened?
  3. Does an AI answer feel more believable when it sounds confident? What could you check first?
  4. Which thinking trap do you fall into most often: first impressions, only seeking agreement, or overconfidence?
  5. What is one habit you will use to double-check an AI answer this week?

The big idea

Think critically. Question confidently. Use AI wisely. Treat AI as a thinking partner: it can speed up ideas and drafts, but you stay responsible for checking the facts and making the final call.
